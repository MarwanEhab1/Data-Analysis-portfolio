# Sales Analytics Dashboard (Excel)
This project presents a comprehensive Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel. It is designed to monitor and analyze sales performance across time, geographies, categories, and payment methods, helping decision-makers gain valuable business insights.

### Project Objectives:
Track key sales performance indicators (KPIs)
Analyze trends in revenue, cost, and profit
Visualize sales distribution across products, countries, and payment methods
Provide actionable insights to improve business outcomes

### Key Features:
  ### KPIs:
   - Total Sales
   - Total Costs
   - Net Profit
   - Total Orders
   - Orders Breakdown: Completed vs. Returned
### Visual Insights:
Geographic Revenue Map – Revenue by country/region
Category-wise Revenue, Cost & Profit – Bar chart comparison
Payment Method Distribution – Pie chart
Monthly Trends – Revenue, Cost, and Profit per month (Line chart)
Daily Revenue Trends – Revenue by weekday (Bar chart)
### Interactive Filters:
Filter by Year (2024, 2025)
Filter by Month
Filter by Country
Filter by Product Category
### Sales Entry Form:
A user-friendly Sales Form to input and track new sales data.

### Data Enhancements:
Additional calculated columns to enrich analysis:
Year / Month / Day
Delivery Time
Total Cost
Sales Revenue
Net Profit

### Summary Statistics:
-- Included descriptive statistics for:
-- Delivery Time
--Total Costs
-- Sales Revenue
-- Net Profit
-- Quantity
-- Unit Price

### Insights & Recommendations:
Peak performance months and drop in December explained
Recommendations to boost sales in December (e.g., limited-time offers, personalized emails)
Emphasis on excellent customer service and reward strategies to increase retention

### Tools Used:
-- Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)
-- Pivot Tables & Charts
-- Slicers (Interactive Filters)
-- Formulas & Derived Columns
