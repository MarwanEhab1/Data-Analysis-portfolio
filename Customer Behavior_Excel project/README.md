# Customer & Sales Behavior Analytics (Excel Pivot Dashboard) Project Overview
This project analyzes customer behavior, order distribution, and sales performance using Excel Pivot Tables and interactive dashboards.
The analysis spans various dimensions such as location, customer segment, product category, payment methods, and more.
The project is presented in three separate dashboards:
- Sales & Revenue Dashboard
- Order Distribution Dashboard
- Customer Behavior Dashboard

### Key Business Questions Answered
  ## Sales Performance
    -- Which locations generate the highest total sales?
    -- Which product categories contribute the most to total revenue?
    -- Which seasons see the highest and lowest sales activity?
    -- Top 10 products by purchase amount?
    -- How does applying discounts affect the total sales amount?
    -- Which size generates the highest total sales?
    -- What is the average purchase amount by payment method?
 ## Order Distribution
    -- Total orders by location, season, category, shipping type, and payment method
    -- Orders by frequency of purchases
    -- Orders by customer age segment
    -- Total number of orders overall
 ## Customer Behavior & Segmentation
    -- How are customers distributed by subscription status?
    -- What is the average purchase amount per customer segment?
    -- What is the average review rating per segment?
    -- Which customer segment uses promo codes the most?
    -- Which shipping types are most common among VIP customers?
    -- What are the preferred payment methods by customer segment?
    -- Total number of VIP customers
    -- Percentage of promo code usage
    -- Average review rating

- Each dashboard includes slicers and filters for interactive insights.
