# Project Name: 2023 Sales Performance Analysis 

### Project Description:
In this project, I analyzed sales data for the year 2023. The dataset was extracted from an Excel .
xlsm file that contained product-level sales records (orders, prices, categories, quantities, and revenues). 
I performed data cleaning and exploratory analysis to uncover trends, top-performing products, and seasonal patterns. 
I also provided data-driven recommendations to boost revenue, especially during underperforming periods like December.

### Step 1: Data Cleaning in Excel
Separated Product-Category into two columns: Product and Category
Converted data types (Price, Quantity, Total Revenue) to numerical
Handled missing values in the Total column by calculating Price × Quantity
Fixed any date formatting issues in Order Date
Ensured consistency in product/category naming

### Step 2: Data Analysis in Excel
1. Top Revenue Generating Products
Used a pivot table to calculate total revenue per product
Ranked products by revenue in descending order
2. Top 10 Most Expensive Products (by unit price)
Sorted the unique products by Price per Unit
3. Revenue by Category
Grouped products by category
Aggregated revenue to determine top-performing categories
4. Quantity Sold per Category
Summed up Quantity grouped by Category
5. Monthly Revenue Trends
Extracted month from Order Date
Aggregated total revenue per month
Identified peak and low sales months
6. Revenue Summary Stats
Average Revenue per order
Max Revenue in a single order

### Key Findings:
Smartphones and Laptops generated the most revenue
Electronics was the top-performing category
October and November had the highest revenue — possibly due to promotions
December showed a noticeable drop in revenue

### Recommendations:
-- Increase Revenue
Launch limited-time offers in December to attract last-minute shoppers
Promote gift card purchases for holiday gifting
Run personalized email campaigns with product recommendations
Offer free/express shipping for urgent holiday orders
 Enhance Customer Retention
Reward repeat buyers with discounts or points
Provide excellent post-sale support to increase satisfaction and loyalty
-- On December Revenue Drop
Possibly due to public holidays affecting operations
Missed marketing opportunities (vs. October/November)
