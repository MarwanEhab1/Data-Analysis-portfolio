#  Sales Analytics Dashboard (Excel Pivot Tables)

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes electronic sales data using Excel Pivot Tables and dashboards.  
The goal is to uncover key business insights related to products, customers, and sales performance between  2023 and  2024.

The analysis is divided into two dashboards:
-  Customer Dashboard  
-  Product Dashboard

---

##  Key Business Questions Answered

###  Product Insights
- Which product has the highest sales?
- What is the unit price for each product?
- What is the most canceled product?
- What percentage of total revenue comes from add-ons?
- How much revenue is lost due to cancellations?
- What is the top product by quantity sold?
- What is the best-selling product by age segment?

###  Customer Insights
- What is the average age of customers?
- What is the minimum customer age?
- Which age segment generates the highest sales?
- Which gender makes the most purchases?
- What is the most used shipping type?
- What is the most used payment method?
- What is the average customer rating by shipping type?
- What are the customer satisfaction levels by rating?

---

##  Tools & Techniques Used
- ✅ Microsoft Excel
- ✅ Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- ✅ Data Cleaning (null removal, value standardization)
- ✅ Calculated Columns (age segments, revenue %, order status)

##  Dashboard Highlights
- Interactive slicers for filtering by gender, age, and shipping type
- Clear visual titles on each chart (in question format)
